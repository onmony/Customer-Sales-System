# Workspace Personalization

Workspace Personalization is a product capability, not merely a UI feature.

The ERP should adapt to professionals.

Professionals should not repeatedly adapt to the ERP.

## 1. Philosophy

Professionals use this software every day.

The application should remember how each individual prefers to work.

The goal is to reduce repetitive actions and operational friction.

The workspace should feel personally optimized without changing business rules.

## 2. Personalization Principles

These principles are permanent:

*   Workspace adapts to the professional.
*   Business workflows never change.
*   Only presentation changes.
*   Personalization must never affect business rules.
*   Personalization must never affect other users.
*   Every preference belongs only to the logged-in user.

## 3. Remembered Preferences

The workspace may remember user preferences across multiple areas.

### Layout

*   Expanded sections.
*   Collapsed sections.
*   Panel sizes.
*   Splitter positions.
*   Workspace Accordion state.

### Tables

*   Column order.
*   Column widths.
*   Hidden columns.
*   Sorting.
*   Filters.
*   Density mode.

### Workspace

*   Active tab.
*   Default view.
*   Preferred grouping.
*   Preferred summaries.

### Navigation

*   Recently opened customers.
*   Recently opened orders.
*   Favourite workspaces.

### Search

*   Recent searches.
*   Saved searches.

## 4. Workspace Behaviour

First-time users receive the recommended workspace.

Experienced users receive their personalized workspace automatically.

The system should never require users to repeatedly configure the same workspace.

## 5. Persistence

Preferences should:

*   Persist across sessions.
*   Persist across devices.
*   Be tied to the authenticated user.
*   Never modify shared business data.
*   Include expanded and collapsed Workspace Accordion sections as a standard capability.

## 6. Workspace Density

Workspaces should support multiple density modes.

Examples:

*   Comfortable.
*   Compact.
*   High Density.

Users should choose the density that matches their work style.

## 7. Collapsible Sections

Business sections may be collapsed where appropriate.

Examples:

*   Customer Overview.
*   Business Metrics.
*   Pricing.
*   Contracts.
*   Notes.
*   Timeline.
*   History.
*   Transactions.

The application remembers the user's choices.

## 8. Tables

Users may personalize table presentation.

Examples:

*   Column order.
*   Column visibility.
*   Width.
*   Sorting.
*   Filters.
*   Grouping.
*   Page size.

These preferences belong to the user.

## 9. Keyboard Productivity

Personalization should improve keyboard efficiency.

Examples:

*   Default focus.
*   Preferred shortcuts.
*   Frequently used actions.
*   Recently used commands.

## 10. Product Principles

These principles are permanent:

*   The workspace adapts to the professional.
*   Business workflows remain consistent.
*   Users personalize presentation, never business behaviour.
*   Personalization reduces operational friction.
*   The system remembers how professionals prefer to work.

## 11. Future Scope

Future modules inherit these principles automatically.

Examples:

*   Customer.
*   Pricing.
*   Order.
*   Invoice.
*   Payment.
*   Inventory.
*   Warehouse.
*   Reports.
*   Dashboards.
*   Administration.
