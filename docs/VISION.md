# Vision

## Product Vision

This project is a modern SaaS ERP focused on customer relationship operations.

The product revolves around customers. Accounting is one module, not the product center.

## First Goal

The first goal is to create the fastest order-to-delivery platform with customer-specific pricing.

The first goal is not to replace Tally.

## Primary Workflow

Customer -> Order -> Pricing Resolution -> Invoice -> Warehouse -> Shipment -> Delivery -> Payment

## Core Business Capability

Customer-specific pricing is the primary business capability.

Every customer may have different pricing for every product. Pricing is versioned. History must never be lost.
