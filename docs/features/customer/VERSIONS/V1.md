# Customer V1

## Purpose

Define the minimum customer capability required to support Phase 1 order-to-invoice workflows.

This document is the source of truth for Customer V1 business rules. Technical documents must reference these rules instead of redefining them.

## Included Scope

- Create customer.
- View customer.
- Search customer.
- Select customer for order creation.
- Store customer contact and address information.
- Preserve downstream customer snapshots.
- Enforce tenant isolation.

## Excluded Scope

- Credit limits.
- Segmentation.
- Customer scoring.
- AI duplicate detection.
- Advanced account hierarchy.
- Customer hierarchy.
- Loyalty programs.
- Payment terms.
- Collections workflow.
- Customer portal access.

## Business Rules

### CUS-1 Tenant Scope

Every customer belongs to exactly one tenant.

Customers from one tenant must not be visible, searchable, selectable, or usable by another tenant.

### CUS-2 Customer Identity

Customer identity must be clear enough for users to distinguish one customer from another.

### CUS-3 Customer Creation

A user may create a customer when the customer does not already exist in the current tenant.

Required customer fields are not finalized in V1 and must be approved before implementation.

### CUS-4 Customer Search

A user must be able to search customers in the current tenant before creating an order.

### CUS-5 Customer Selection For Order

An order must be associated with one customer.

### CUS-6 Customer Contacts

A customer may have contact information used for sales and operations.

Required contact fields are not finalized in V1.

### CUS-7 Customer Addresses

A customer may have address information used for invoicing, shipment, and delivery.

Required address fields are not finalized in V1.

### CUS-8 Historical Snapshot Safety

Changes to customer information must not rewrite immutable order or invoice snapshots.

### CUS-9 Duplicate Customer Handling

Duplicate customer behavior is not finalized in V1.

The system must not invent whether duplicates should be blocked, warned, merged, or allowed.

### CUS-10 Module Boundary

Customer defines customer identity and customer context. It does not define pricing rules, product rules, invoice numbering, payment collection, shipment policy, or accounting behavior.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A user can find an existing customer before creating an order.
- A user can create a customer when the customer does not exist.
- A customer from another tenant is not visible.
- A user can select a customer before creating an order.
- Customer changes do not alter historical order or invoice snapshots.
- Duplicate handling remains blocked as a business decision until approved.

## Open Questions

- Required customer fields are not finalized.
- Customer status list is not finalized.
- Duplicate handling policy is not finalized.
- Required contact fields are not finalized.
- Required address fields are not finalized.
- Customer naming policy is not finalized.
- Customer code or numbering policy is not finalized.
