# Pricing V1

## Purpose

Define manual customer-specific pricing for Phase 1 order creation.

This document is the source of truth for Pricing V1 business rules. Technical documents must reference these rules instead of redefining them.

## Included Scope

- Create customer-specific price.
- Change customer-specific price by creating a new version.
- Resolve customer-specific price for an order item.
- Store resolved price snapshot on order item.

## Excluded Scope

- Bulk import.
- Pricing templates.
- Promotions.
- Discount schemes.
- AI pricing suggestions.
- Currency conversion.
- Contract pricing.
- Approval workflow for pricing changes.
- Tax calculation.
- Product master pricing strategy.

## Business Rules

### PRI-1 Tenant Scope

Every pricing record belongs to exactly one tenant.

Pricing records from one tenant must not be visible, searchable, resolvable, or usable by another tenant.

### PRI-2 Customer-Specific Price

Every customer may have a unique price for every product.

### PRI-3 Customer And Product Required

A customer-specific price must be associated with one customer and one product in the same tenant.

### PRI-4 Versioned Changes

Changing a customer-specific price creates a new pricing version.

The previous version must remain available for history.

### PRI-5 History Preservation

Pricing history must never be deleted as part of normal business operation.

### PRI-6 Current Price Resolution

Pricing is resolved when a product is added to an order for a customer.

### PRI-7 Missing Customer Price

If no customer-specific price exists, V1 must not invent fallback behavior.

The missing-price policy must be approved before implementation.

### PRI-8 Immutable Order Snapshot

Saved order items must store the resolved price as an immutable snapshot.

### PRI-9 Future Price Changes

Changing a customer-specific price after an order is saved must not change the saved order item price.

### PRI-10 Traceability

A saved order item pricing snapshot must be traceable to the pricing version used during order creation.

### PRI-11 Pricing Boundary

Pricing determines customer-product price. It does not define product identity, customer identity, tax policy, invoice numbering, inventory, shipment, or payment rules.

## Decision Table Mapping

- PRI-8, PRI-9, and PRI-10 map to [Saved Order After Price Change](../DECISION_TABLES.md#decision-table-3-saved-order-after-price-change).

## Acceptance Criteria

- A user can define a customer-specific price.
- A user can resolve pricing during order creation.
- A saved order price remains unchanged after later price changes.
- Cross-tenant prices are not visible or usable.
- Pricing history remains available after a price change.
- Missing-price behavior remains blocked until approved.

## Open Questions

- Missing customer-specific price behavior is not finalized.
- Pricing permissions are not finalized.
- Effective date policy is not finalized.
- Price change reason policy is not finalized.
- Price precision and rounding policy are not finalized.
- Currency policy is not finalized.
