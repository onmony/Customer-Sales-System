# Invoice V1

## Purpose

Define minimum invoice capability for Phase 1 order-to-invoice workflow.

## Included Scope

- Create invoice from saved order.
- Preserve invoice snapshots.
- Mark invoice as issued.
- Prevent changes to issued invoices.

## Excluded Scope

- Payments.
- Tax filing.
- Credit notes.
- E-invoicing.
- Accounting ledger posting.
- Invoice reminders.

## Business Rules

### INV-1 Tenant Scope

Every invoice belongs to one tenant.

### INV-2 Source Order Required

An invoice must be created from a saved order.

### INV-3 Snapshot Required

Invoice items must preserve required customer, product, quantity, and pricing values.

### INV-4 Issued Invoice Immutability

An issued invoice must not be changed.

### INV-5 Historical Safety

Later customer, product, pricing, or order changes must not rewrite an issued invoice.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A user can create an invoice from a saved order.
- Invoice items preserve order item pricing.
- Issued invoices cannot be edited.
- Cross-tenant invoices are not visible.

## Open Questions

- Invoice numbering is not finalized.
- Draft invoice behavior is not finalized.
- Cancellation and credit-note policy is not finalized.
- Invoice status list is not finalized.
