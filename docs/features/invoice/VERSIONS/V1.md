# Invoice V1

## Purpose

Define minimum invoice capability for Phase 1 order-to-invoice workflow.

This document is the source of truth for Invoice V1 business rules. Technical documents must reference these rules instead of redefining them.

## Included Scope

- Create invoice from saved order.
- Preserve invoice snapshots.
- Mark invoice as issued.
- Prevent changes to issued invoices.
- Enforce tenant isolation.
- Validate order invoice readiness.

## Excluded Scope

- Payments.
- Tax filing.
- Credit notes.
- E-invoicing.
- Accounting ledger posting.
- Invoice reminders.
- Recurring invoices.
- Partial invoicing.
- Invoice discounts.
- Invoice templates.
- Collections workflow.
- Refunds.

## Business Rules

### INV-1 Tenant Scope

Every invoice belongs to exactly one tenant.

Invoices from one tenant must not be visible, searchable, selectable, or usable by another tenant.

### INV-2 Source Order Required

An invoice must be created from a saved order.

The source order must belong to the same tenant as the invoice.

### INV-3 Order Invoice Readiness

An invoice can be created only from an order that is invoice-ready according to the Order module.

### INV-4 Invoice Items Required

An invoice must contain invoice items derived from source order items.

### INV-5 Snapshot Required

Invoice items must preserve required customer, product, quantity, and pricing values.

### INV-6 Issue Invoice

An invoice may be issued after required invoice data and snapshots exist.

Invoice issue preconditions beyond source order readiness are not finalized in V1.

### INV-7 Issued Invoice Immutability

An issued invoice must not be changed.

### INV-8 Historical Safety

Later customer, product, pricing, or order changes must not rewrite an issued invoice.

### INV-9 Draft Invoice Policy

Draft invoice behavior is not finalized in V1.

The system must not invent whether draft invoices exist, can be edited, expire, or reserve numbers.

### INV-10 Invoice Numbering Policy

Invoice numbering is not finalized in V1.

The system must not invent numbering format, sequencing, prefixes, resets, or tenant-specific numbering behavior.

### INV-11 Cancellation And Credit Policy

Invoice cancellation, voiding, credit notes, and reversal behavior are not finalized in V1.

The system must not invent correction behavior.

### INV-12 Module Boundary

Invoice defines invoice creation, snapshots, issue behavior, and immutability. It does not define payment collection, tax filing, accounting ledger posting, e-invoicing, shipment, delivery, or pricing calculation.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A user can create an invoice from a saved order.
- Invoice items preserve order item pricing.
- Issued invoices cannot be edited.
- Cross-tenant invoices are not visible.
- Invoice creation is blocked when the source order is not invoice-ready.
- Later customer, product, pricing, or order changes do not alter an issued invoice.
- Draft, numbering, cancellation, and credit-note behavior remains blocked until approved.

## Open Questions

- Invoice numbering is not finalized.
- Draft invoice behavior is not finalized.
- Cancellation and credit-note policy is not finalized.
- Invoice status list is not finalized.
- Invoice issue preconditions are not finalized.
- Partial invoicing policy is not finalized.
- Tax display behavior is not finalized.
- Invoice print or export format is not finalized.
