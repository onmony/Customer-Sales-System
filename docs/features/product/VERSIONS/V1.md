# Product V1

## Purpose

Define the minimum product capability required for Phase 1 order and pricing workflows.

This document is the source of truth for Product V1 business rules. Technical documents must reference these rules instead of redefining them.

## Included Scope

- Create product.
- View product.
- Search product.
- Select product during order creation.
- Preserve downstream product snapshots.
- Enforce tenant isolation.

## Excluded Scope

- Inventory quantities.
- Product variants.
- Bills of materials.
- Product bundles.
- Advanced tax classification.
- Barcode scanning.
- Serial number tracking.
- Batch or lot tracking.
- Warehouse allocation.
- Product images.
- Product categories.

## Business Rules

### PRO-1 Tenant Scope

Every product belongs to exactly one tenant.

Products from one tenant must not be visible, searchable, selectable, or usable by another tenant.

### PRO-2 Product Identity

Product identity must be clear enough for users to distinguish one product from another.

### PRO-3 Product Creation

A user may create a product when the product does not already exist in the current tenant.

Required product fields are not finalized in V1 and must be approved before implementation.

### PRO-4 Product Search

A user must be able to search products in the current tenant before adding them to an order.

### PRO-5 Product Selection For Order

Only products in the current tenant can be selected for orders.

### PRO-6 Product Unit

A product may have a unit used for order quantity and invoice display.

Unit-of-measure policy is not finalized in V1.

### PRO-7 Snapshot Safety

Product details required by immutable business documents must be snapshotted when needed.

### PRO-8 Inactive Product Behavior

Inactive product behavior is not finalized in V1.

The system must not invent whether inactive products can be ordered, priced, or displayed outside historical documents.

### PRO-9 Pricing Boundary

Customer-specific pricing is documented in the Pricing module.

### PRO-10 Module Boundary

Product defines product identity and product context. It does not define customer-specific pricing, inventory quantity, warehouse allocation, shipment, delivery, payment, or accounting behavior.

## Acceptance Criteria

- A user can search products in the current tenant.
- A user can add a product to an order.
- Products from another tenant are not visible.
- Product changes do not alter historical order or invoice snapshots.
- Required-field behavior remains blocked until approved.
- Inactive product behavior remains blocked until approved.

## Open Questions

- Required product fields are not finalized.
- Product inactive behavior is not finalized.
- Unit-of-measure policy is not finalized.
- Product naming policy is not finalized.
- Product code or SKU policy is not finalized.
- Product category behavior is not finalized.
