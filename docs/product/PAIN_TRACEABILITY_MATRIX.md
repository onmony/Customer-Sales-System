# Pain Traceability Matrix

This matrix ensures that every module and feature implemented directly addresses a documented customer pain point. It serves as an active enforcement mechanism for our Product Foundation.

| Customer Pain | Product Modules | UX Principles | Reports & Dashboards | Automation / AI |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fragmented Information | Customer, Product, Pricing, Order | Unified Workspace | Dashboard | AI Search |
| Owner Becomes the Operating System | Pricing, Order | Guided Workflows | Alerts | AI Recommendations |
| Duplicate Data Entry | Customer, Order, Invoice | Smart Forms | — | Auto-fill |
| Lack of Business Visibility | Dashboard, Reports | Workspace Summaries | KPIs | AI Insights |
| Business History Depends on Memory | Order, Invoice, Payment | Timeline | Audit Reports | AI Explanation |
