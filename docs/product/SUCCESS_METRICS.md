# Success Metrics

Product success is defined by measurable operational excellence and user efficiency. We track these metrics to ensure the product continually serves its core purpose.

### Operational Speed
- **Create Customer:** Can be completed in under 60 seconds.
- **Create Order:** Can be completed in under 2 minutes.
- **Generate Invoice:** Can be completed in under 30 seconds.

### Interaction Efficiency
- **Minimal Clicks:** Common daily tasks require no more than 2–3 clicks from the user's starting point.
- **Keyboard Dominance:** Heavy users can perform the vast majority of their daily operations without ever leaving the keyboard.
- **Experience Curve:** The system is designed such that users demonstrably become faster and more efficient as they gain experience with the tool.

### Clarity and Onboarding
- **Explainability:** 100% of automatic business decisions made by the system are clearly explained in the UI (What, Why, Rule, Next Steps).
- **Rapid Onboarding:** The workflows are intuitive enough that new employees can become fully productive on the system within their first day on the job.
